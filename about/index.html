Financial services that work in Nigeria

Maria Oyinlola Limited is the financial services arm of OA Group Nigeria — established to provide Nigerians with access to regulated, professionally delivered credit, investment, advisory, and digital financing solutions.

Who We Are

The financing engine of OA Group

Maria Oyinlola Limited (MOL) was established as the financial services company within OA Group Nigeria — providing the group's ecosystem with a regulated financing capability that complements the group's professional services, consulting, real estate, and contracting arms.

Within the OA Group ecosystem, MOL plays a specific financing role: providing working capital for clients implementing advisory mandates (via OA Consulting), financing property transactions (via Henrisol Properties), and providing institutional financial infrastructure for the group's broader commercial activities (via Pearl Primus).

As a CBN-regulated institution, MOL operates within a defined regulatory framework — providing clients with the assurance of regulatory oversight, data protection compliance, and institutional accountability that Nigerian financial services consumers are entitled to expect.

Our Principles

How we approach financial services

Regulatory compliance first

Every MOL product, pricing decision, and client interaction operates within CBN regulatory requirements. Compliance is not a constraint on our business — it is the foundation of the trust our clients place in us.

Transparent pricing

All MOL products carry full disclosure of rates, fees, charges, and conditions before any product is taken up. No hidden charges, no misleading indicative rates. Financial services clients deserve clarity.

Nigerian market specificity

Our products are designed for the Nigerian economic environment — naira volatility, credit infrastructure, and the specific financial challenges that Nigerians navigate. Imported financial product structures are adapted, not applied wholesale.

Client protection

We operate in full compliance with the CBN Consumer Protection Framework. Client complaints are resolved through a structured process. Data is protected under NDPR and NDPA. Clients have rights — and MOL takes them seriously.

Work with a regulated financial services institution

Whether you need credit, want to grow your money, or need financial advisory — MOL combines regulatory standing with practical Nigerian financial market experience.
