Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Know Your Customer (KYC)

MOL is committed to full compliance with Nigeria's anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing (AML/CFT) regulatory framework, including the:

  • Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022
  • Terrorism (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022
  • Central Bank of Nigeria AML/CFT Regulations
  • NFIU (Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit) guidelines

All clients are subject to customer due diligence (CDD) procedures at onboarding and throughout the client relationship. MOL maintains ongoing transaction monitoring, suspicious transaction reporting obligations, and annual AML training for all relevant staff.

KYC Requirements — Individuals

  • Valid government-issued ID (National ID, International Passport, Driver's Licence, Voter's Card)
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN)
  • Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement — not older than 3 months)
  • Passport photograph

KYC Requirements — Businesses

  • Certificate of Incorporation (CAC)
  • Memorandum and Articles of Association
  • Board resolution authorising account opening
  • KYC documentation for all directors and beneficial owners
  • Proof of business address
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN)

Consumer Protection & Client Rights

MOL is subject to the CBN Consumer Protection Framework and is committed to the following client rights:

  • Right to information: All product terms, interest rates, fees, and charges are disclosed clearly before any product is taken up
  • Right to fair treatment: No discriminatory treatment in product access or pricing
  • Right to recourse: A structured complaint resolution process with defined timelines
  • Right to data protection: Client data is handled in accordance with the NDPR 2019 and NDPA 2023

Complaint Resolution Process

If you have a complaint about any MOL product or service:

  • Step 1 — Contact MOL directly: info@mol.oagroupng.com, 0803 092 5999 or 0805 381 6665
  • Step 2 — We will acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours and resolve within 7 business days
  • Step 3 — If unresolved, escalate to the CBN Consumer Protection Department at www.cbn.gov.ng

Data Protection Compliance

MOL processes personal and financial data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. Our data protection obligations include:

  • Lawful basis for all personal data processing activities
  • Explicit consent for marketing communications
  • Data minimisation and retention policies aligned with regulatory requirements
  • Secure data storage with encryption at rest and in transit
  • Annual NDPR audit and compliance reporting
